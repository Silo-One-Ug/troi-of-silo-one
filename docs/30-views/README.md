Five surfaces fill the whole window, edge to edge, with the bar floating over them. They are
canvases of their own and keep every gesture they have; the agent can open any of them by voice
("switch to the hands", "show me my files", "open the vault"), and on the phone they open from the
**Views** tab, with a pill at the bottom to come back.

| View | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Face** | The agent's face, which moves as it speaks and listens |
| [Hands](hands-board.md) | The hand-tracked glass board: where the agent puts up pictures, cards, notes and 3D models |
| [Files](files-galaxy.md) | A folder drawn as a galaxy: folders as nuclei, files as stars |
| [Apps](apps-galaxy.md) | Every application as a star, grouped by category; running apps glow |
| [Vault](vault.md) | The agent's memory as a wall of cards, drawn by the same engine as the galaxies |

## Hand gestures

The galaxies, the Vault wall and the board can be worked with your hands in front of the webcam,
once the Hands switch is on ([The board](hands-board.md) has the gesture table). The mouse and
the trackpad always work; on the phone, touch does.
