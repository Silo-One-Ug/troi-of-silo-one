Every application as a standalone icon, grouped by category; running apps glow. Tap (or dwell with a
hand) to open one. A long press offers **Open** or **Bring to front**, **Quit**, **Force quit** (an
approval) and **Show in Finder**.

The categories come from each app's own declaration, with System or Other as the fallback, and the
icons are the apps' own. The list refreshes itself while the galaxy is open, so an app you start
glows within a moment.

By voice: "open the apps" shows the galaxy; "open Spotify", "close Chrome" and "what's running" go
straight to the app, through [`apps.sh`](../40-abilities/files-and-apps.md). A quit is polite (the app
may ask you to save); a force quit only files a request for your approval.
