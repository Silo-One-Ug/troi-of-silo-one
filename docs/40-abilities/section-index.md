Troi is not a chatbot with a keyboard. It has a browser, native hands on every app, a camera and
screen sight, application control, file hands, its own console and galaxies, the hands board,
reminders, the calendar, your Google account, meetings, the news, pictures, music, code sessions, CI
and servers, Telegram and phone calls. When a request needs the world, it reaches for the tool
instead of saying it cannot.

| Page | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| [Files and apps](files-and-apps.md) | Moving, renaming and organising inside your file roots; opening, focusing and quitting apps; trash and force-quit requests |
| [The native hands](native-hands.md) | Every app by name through the accessibility tree: press, set, menu, type, wait; the guards; the cursor for what has no name |
| [The browser](the-browser.md) | Arc, or a Chrome of the agent's own; what it may do on a site; the Browser page |
| [Eyes and screen](eyes-and-screen.md) | A look through the camera or at the screen, the sight modes, watch mode |
| [Pictures](pictures.md) | Research with credited pictures on the board; making pictures from words; showing a picture |
| [News](news.md) | Headlines per outlet, Uganda first; reading an article; an outlet's videos and social pages |
| [Music and movies](music-and-movies.md) | Apple Music by voice; movie mode |
| [Meetings](meetings.md) | Recording a meeting, the speaker-labelled transcript, the meeting note |
| [Reminders and calendar](reminders-and-calendar.md) | Reminders that fire wherever you are; the Mac's Calendar |
| [Briefs and reports](briefs-and-reports.md) | The morning brief and the end-of-day report |

| [Your Google account](google.md) | Calendar, Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Tasks and Contacts, signed in once; what needs your go-ahead |

The exact commands behind each ability are in the [toolbox](../70-reference/toolbox.md).
