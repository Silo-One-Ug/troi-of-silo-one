## Apple Music

"Play some jazz", "play my gospel playlist", "pause", "next", "what's this song", "louder",
"quieter", "volume 40", "shuffle on", "I like this one": the agent drives Apple Music. A playlist is
found by name, then the library by genre, artist, album or title. When nothing matches, the app's
search opens for you to tap.

With `duck_music` on in the settings, music dips while the agent speaks.

## Movie mode

"Movie mode": push to talk, so the film cannot wake the agent; the sound cues off; the floating
conversation hidden; the page dimmed. "Movie mode off" restores it. If you were hands-free before,
say "go hands free" yourself, since that one is protected.
