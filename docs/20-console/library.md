Every picture the agent took, made, found or received, shelved as books by source and by day:
camera looks, screenshots, research pictures, pictures it made, Telegram photos, the board, uploads.

- **Filter** by source and **search**.
- **Open a book** and view a picture full size (← / → to move, Esc to close).
- **Star** the ones to keep past the 14-day tidy-up. Camera looks, screenshots and Telegram files
  older than 14 days go to the macOS Trash at night unless starred; their rows stay in the record,
  marked archived ([Housekeeping](../60-memory/housekeeping.md)).
- **Delete.** The bin on a picture sends it to the macOS Trash (recoverable there) and marks its
  row archived; the bin on a book takes the whole book. **Select** in a book turns the tiles into
  checkboxes, with **Select all** and one **Delete** for the lot. Every deletion asks first and
  needs you, as an approval does: an unlocked console (your PIN, or your face). The agent has no
  bin of its own; its trash requests still go through Approvals.

The pictures live in `data/blobs/<kind>/` and are indexed in the record. "Open the images" or "open
the library" brings the page up by voice.
