The brain delegates when a job is big enough: a survey of files, a piece of research, a review, a
long read. Each delegation is an agent of its own, with its own context, working while the brain
carries on. The **Agents** page shows them as a chart: the brain at the top, the agents it has sent
below, the live ones ringed in green with an **Active** badge, the finished ones dimmed with how long
they took.

- **Named for the job.** The brain names each agent for what it is sent to do, a role and the task:
  "Scout: the Commuza notes", "Reviewer: PR 12", "Researcher: NBS on the budget". That name is what
  the chart shows, with the kind of agent under it (scout, planner, hand, guide).
- **What each is doing.** Tap an agent for its task, what it is doing right now (reading a file,
  searching, in the browser), its last words, how many turns it has taken, and its report once it
  has finished. An agent that has not said a word in half an hour shows as **Quiet**.
- **The brain's own line.** The top node shows the brain's state and the tool in its hands, as the
  bar does.
- **Archiving.** A finished agent can be archived off the chart: the × on its node, or **Archive
  finished** for all of them at once; **Show archived** brings them back into view. Nothing is
  deleted: the record of the session is Claude Code's own and stays as it is.
- **In the bar and on the phone.** The Agents icon in the bar carries a count of the agents at
  work; on the phone the page is under **More**. "Open the agents" brings it up by voice.

Nothing on the page is a control: the brain decides whom to send. The page reads Claude Code's own
record of the session, so it shows every delegation of the session, whether it was asked for in the
room, from the phone or by a routine.
