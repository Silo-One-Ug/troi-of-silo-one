title: Agents
description: "Who the brain has put on a task, named for the job, and how each one is doing: a chart with the brain at the top and its delegations below."

---

The brain delegates when a job is big enough: a survey of files, a piece of research, a review, a
long read. Each delegation is an agent of its own, with its own context, working while the brain
carries on. The **Agents** page shows them as a chart: the brain at the top, the agents it has sent
below, the live ones ringed in green with an **Active** badge, the finished ones dimmed with how long
they took.

- **Named for the job.** The brain names each agent for what it is sent to do, a role and the task:
  "Scout: the Commuza notes", "Reviewer: PR 12", "Researcher: NBS on the budget". That name is what
  the chart shows, with the kind of agent under it (scout, planner, hand, guide).
- **The agent's card.** Tap an agent for its card: the role above the task, with an icon for the
  role (a wrench for a fixer, a glass for a scout, a clipboard for a reviewer, a book for a
  researcher, a flask for a tester, a pen for a writer); whether it is at work, finished, failed or
  quiet; the kind of agent and the brain it runs on; when it started, how long it has taken and how
  many turns. While it works, a green line says what it has in its hands, the
  exact command beside the words. Under that, its last steps on a rail, said the way the
  conversation says the brain's own (at the terminal, reading a file, in the repository), its latest
  words, and, once it has finished, its report, rendered, with a copy button. The task it was given
  sits last, whole, and folds to two lines if you want it out of the way. An agent's report never
  lands in the conversation: it comes back to the brain, which answers in its own words; the report
  itself lives here.
- **Every node on a line.** The brain runs down to a bus, the bus across the first row and a stub
  down to each agent. When the row wraps, every further row has a bus of its own, fed by a rail down
  the left of the chart, so no agent is ever left hanging. An agent can send agents too: its own
  delegations hang under it on a short rail of their own, three levels deep, each with its card.
- **The brain's own line.** The top node shows the brain's state and the tool in its hands, as the
  bar does.
- **Archiving.** A finished agent can be archived off the chart: the × on its node, or **Archive
  finished** for all of them at once; **Show archived** brings them back into view. Nothing is
  deleted: the record of the session is Claude Code's own and stays as it is.
- **In the bar and on the phone.** The Agents icon in the bar carries a count of the agents at
  work; on the phone the page is under **More**. "Open the agents" brings it up by voice.

Nothing on the page is a control: the brain decides whom to send. The page reads Claude Code's own
record of the session, so it shows every delegation of the session, whether it was asked for in the
room, from the phone or by a routine.
