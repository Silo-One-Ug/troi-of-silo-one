Five promises, each kept by code rather than by good behaviour:

1. **Troi never deletes.** A hook blocks every destructive command, whatever the permission mode.
   Trash, force-quit and risky clicks need your approval, and what you approve goes to the macOS
   Trash.
2. **Only you change how it behaves.** The switches that lower its guard go through only in your
   verified voice or face, from a console you unlocked, or with your Telegram approval.
3. **It cannot reach its own safeguards.** The Keychain, approvals and unlocks, the settings file,
   the prints, the record, its boot file, Claude Code's settings and the guard's code are yours to
   change, never its; the same hook blocks the attempt.
4. **Its senses stay yours.** The camera is off until you switch it on; what it sees stays on the
   Mac.
5. **Strangers get hospitality, not access.** Your files, accounts, messages and whereabouts stay
   yours, whoever is at the machine.
